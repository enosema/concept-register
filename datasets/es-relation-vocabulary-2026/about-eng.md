# ES Relation Vocabulary (2026)

The controlled vocabulary of concept relation types of the Enosema
Foundation, defined in EFS 2510:2026 (Concept systems and concept
relations), clause 6, and registered under the FERIN framework
(ISO 19135 procedures).

Each relation type is a registered item with a stable URN
(`urn:enosema:relation-type:<identifier>`), a definition, an inverse
(or the symmetric note), its family, and its ISO source. Use the
identifiers exactly as registered; propose additions through the ES
standardization process (EFD 101, EFS 2513).
